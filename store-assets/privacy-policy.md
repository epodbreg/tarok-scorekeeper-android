# Privacy Policy — Štih (Tarok Scorekeeper)

*Last updated: 7 July 2026*

Štih is a score-keeping app for the card game tarok.

**Data collection: none.** The app does not collect, transmit, store remotely, or share any personal data. It has no internet permission, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

All data you enter (player names, game scores, session history, settings) is stored **only locally on your device** and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

CSV exports are created only when you explicitly use the export function, and are shared only through the app you choose in the system share dialog.

# Politika zasebnosti — Štih

*Zadnja posodobitev: 7. julij 2026*

Štih je aplikacija za beleženje točk pri taroku.

**Zbiranje podatkov: nič.** Aplikacija ne zbira, ne pošilja in ne deli nobenih osebnih podatkov. Nima dostopa do interneta, nima analitike in nima oglasov.
